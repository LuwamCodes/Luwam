Discover Traditional Eritrean Food

Introduction

Eritrean food is delicious and full of flavour. Eritrea have nine ethnic group and each ethnicity has different traditional dishes.

Eritrean people enjoy different traditional dishes made with fresh ingredients, vegetables, meat and special spices. Injera is one of the most popular traditional foods. It is a soft, sour flatbread usually made from teff flour. It is served with different stews and sauces.

Popular Eritrean Traditional Foods

There are many delicious traditional dishes in Eritrea. Here are three popular examples:

  • Zigni – a spicy meat stew served with injera.
  • Tsebhi Dorho – a traditional chicken stew.
  • Hilbet – a traditional Eritrean dish made from ground legumes, usually served with injera.
